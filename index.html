Quick Note

This link/pilot test is only for friends and family right now, and on that note, all requests/prints are free. I need ideas for things to print, so feel free to submit creative, useful, or custom ideas.

FYI: Using both PLA and PETG (lots of colors coming soon!)

I may reach out or decline a request if the object is too large, too complex, unsafe/inappropriate, needs a different material, has an unprintable file/model, or is too time intensive or filament-heavy for this pilot test. If you're unsure, submit it anyway and I'll let you know — it's no trouble.

If the print is on the larger side, a small $5–$10 donation would be appreciated to help buy more filament. Do not send any money if your object is small, even if it is complicated or custom.

Thank you — excited to see your ideas.

2. What Do You Want Printed?

Describe the item in plain English. You do not need to know 3D printing terms.

Some simple print ideas:

  • Keychains, name tags, or bag tags
  • Phone stands, controller stands, or headphone hooks
  • Desk organizers, cable clips, or small trays
  • Replacement knobs, caps, brackets, or small household fixes
  • Small decorations, signs, or display pieces

3. Model, Link, Picture, or Custom Design

You do not need to have a 3D model ready. You can send me an idea, a picture, or a link. I can also help search for something similar, or I can try to make a simple custom design.

Helpful places to browse for ideas:

  • MakerWorld (preferred site for ideas)
  • Thingiverse

Tip: Watch out for models that require extra parts like screws, glue, or other hardware. These aren't impossible, but keep in mind they may need to be purchased and assembled.

Allowed attachments: pictures only, such as JPG, PNG, HEIC, or WEBP. Max size: 5 MB each.

4. Size

Give your best guess. If you are not sure, choose a general size and I can help figure it out. As a rough reference, the maximum single-piece print size is about 10 in × 10 in × 10 in. Anything close to that limit, or anything that needs to be printed in multiple pieces and assembled after, should be considered large.

5. Color

I prefer black and white, but several additional colors are coming soon. If you want another color, select it below or enter a custom color and I'll see what I can do.
