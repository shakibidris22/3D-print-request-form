3D Print Request Form

Submit an idea, picture, or link for something you want 3D printed.

Quick Note

This link/pilot test is only for friends and family right now, and on that note, all requests/prints are free. I need ideas for things to print, so feel free to submit creative, useful, or custom ideas.

FYI: I am strictly using PLA for now. PLA is a standard plastic that is fairly durable depending on the shape, size, and design of the object.

I may reach out or decline a request if the object is too large, too complex, unsafe or inappropriate, needs material other than PLA, has an unprintable file/model, or would be too time intensive or use too much filament for this pilot test. If you’re unsure whether your print violates any of these terms, please submit it anyway and I’ll let you know — it’s no trouble.

If the print is on the larger side, a small $5–$10 donation would be appreciated to help buy more filament. Do not send any money if your object is small, even if it is complicated or custom.

Thank you — excited to see your ideas!

1. Contact Info

Tell me who you are so I know who the request is for.

2. What Do You Want Printed?

Describe the item in plain English. You do not need to know 3D printing terms.

Some simple print ideas:

  • Keychains, name tags, or bag tags
  • Phone stands, controller stands, or headphone hooks
  • Desk organizers, cable clips, or small trays
  • Replacement knobs, caps, brackets, or small household fixes
  • Small decorations, signs, or display pieces

3. Model, Link, Picture, or Custom Design

You do not need to have a 3D model ready. You can send me an idea, a picture, or a link. I can also help search for something similar, or I can try to make a simple custom design.

Helpful places to browse for ideas:

  • Printables
  • Thingiverse
  • MakerWorld
  • Thangs

Tip: simple-looking models are usually better for now. Some models online may be unreliable, oversized, or require extra post-processing like screws, sanding, glue, or assembly. If something looks too good to be true, it may be harder to print than it looks.

Allowed attachments: pictures only, such as JPG, PNG, HEIC, or WEBP. Max size: 5 MB each.

4. Size

Give your best guess. If you are not sure, choose a general size and I can help figure it out. As a rough reference, the maximum single-piece print size is about 10 in × 10 in × 10 in. Anything close to that limit, or anything that needs to be printed in multiple pieces and assembled after, should be considered large.

5. Color

For now, I only print in black or white PLA plastic. Potential future colors may include general colors, matte colors, or silk-style filament.

6. Use Details

These questions help me understand whether the object needs to fit, attach, or support something.

7. Deadline

Since I am doing this for practice, flexible timing is appreciated.

8. Extra Notes

Add anything else that might help me understand what you want. Shoutout to whoever actually read all this.
